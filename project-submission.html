What the platform can do

Core tools for planning, coordinating, and managing an event.

Events, dashboard & calendar

Create and switch between events, review a progress dashboard, and see important dates and planning milestones in a calendar.

Tasks & run-of-show timeline

Organize to-dos and deadlines, then prepare a detailed event-day schedule with timing, locations, responsibilities, and notes.

Guests, RSVP & seating

Manage guest details and RSVP status, share a public registration link, review attendance requests, and organize seating assignments.

Budget & vendor management

Track budgets and expenses, organize vendor details and payment milestones, and keep supplier contract files together.

02Project overview

AI planning assistant

Get context-aware help with event questions, budget allocation ideas, schedule drafts, and vendor outreach messages.

Exports & calendar sharing

Export guest and vendor lists, create printable reports and schedules, and share calendar events using the standard ICS format.

Team access & activity

Invite teammates and clients with role-based access, review workspace activity, and manage notifications and account preferences.

Event settings & secure access

Configure event details such as currency, timezone, venue, and banner; use account authentication and database access controls for workspace data.
